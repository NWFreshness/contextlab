# Pricing Plans

## Starter Plan

- **Price:** $9/month
- **Users:** 1 user
- **Features:** Basic access, 100 requests/day, Email support
- **Limits:** 10 projects maximum

## Professional Plan

- **Price:** $29/month
- **Users:** Up to 5 users
- **Features:** Full access, 10,000 requests/day, Priority support
- **Limits:** 100 projects maximum

## Enterprise Plan

- **Price:** $99/month
- **Users:** Unlimited users
- **Features:** Full access, Unlimited requests, Dedicated support, Custom integrations
- **Limits:** No project limits

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Extra Users | $5/user/month | Additional user seats |
| Extra Requests | $10 per 10K | Burst request capacity |
| Custom Domain | $15/month | Branded experience |
| Analytics Plus | $20/month | Advanced reporting |

## Notes

- All plans are billed monthly or annually (annual = 2 months free)
- Prices in USD, excluding applicable taxes
- Enterprise pricing may vary based on negotiated terms
