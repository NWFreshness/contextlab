# Refund Policy v1 (Superseded)

**Status: Superseded by refund_policy_v3.md**

Our refund window was **14 days** from purchase date. To request a refund:

1. Contact support within 14 days
2. Provide order number and reason
3. Items must be unused and in original packaging
4. Refund to original payment method within 5-7 business days

**Exceptions:**
- Final sale items are non-refundable
- Digital products cannot be refunded once downloaded
- Shipping costs are non-refundable

For the current policy, see refund_policy_v3.md.
