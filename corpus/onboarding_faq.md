# Onboarding FAQ

Common questions from new customers.

## Returns and Refunds

**Q: How long can I send something back?**

A: You have a full month (30 days) from when you bought it to initiate a return. Just reach out to our support team within that window with your order number. Items should be in their original condition when possible. The money returns to your original payment method within a few business days after we process it.

**Q: What if I bought something on sale?**

A: Our regular return window applies to sale items too. You still get the full 30 days.

**Q: What can't be returned?**

A: Perishable items, anything marked as final sale, digital products once you've downloaded them, and services we've already completed.

## Shipping

**Q: How long does ground shipping take?**

A: For West Coast addresses, standard ground shipping is 5 business days. East Coast is typically 7-10 business days.

**Q: Can I track my order?**

A: Yes, you'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships.

## Account

**Q: How do I reset my password?**

A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**

A: Contact our support team. Note that we cannot delete accounts with pending orders.
