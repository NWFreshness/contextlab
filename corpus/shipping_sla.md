# Shipping Service Level Agreements

## Standard Shipping

| Region | Service | Transit Time |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| West Coast | Ground | 5 business days |
| East Coast | Ground | 7-10 business days |
| Central | Ground | 5-7 business days |

## Express Shipping

| Region | Service | Transit Time |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| West Coast | 2-Day | 2 business days |
| East Coast | 2-Day | 2 business days |
| National | Overnight | 1 business day |

## Business Days

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. Orders placed after 2 PM local time ship the next business day.

## Tracking

All shipments include tracking. Standard ground includes basic tracking; express and overnight include signature confirmation.

## Remote Areas

Some remote areas may add 1-2 additional business days to all service levels.
