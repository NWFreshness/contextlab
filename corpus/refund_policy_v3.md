# Refund Policy (Current)

**Policy ID: POL-REF-30**

Our refund window is **30 days** from purchase date. We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

## How to Request a Refund

1. Contact our support team within 30 days of purchase
2. Have your order number ready
3. Describe the reason for the refund request
4. Items must be in resalable condition

## Refund Timeline

- Request processed within 2 business days
- Refund to original payment method within 3-5 business days
- You will receive confirmation via email

## Exclusions

- Perishable goods are non-refundable
- Items marked as "final sale"
- Digital downloads once accessed
- Services already rendered

## Exceptions

We may make exceptions for defective products or errors on our end. Contact support with your order number for special consideration.
